## Source-specific OCR scale experiment

The hackathon poster was checked at native, doubled and tripled input scale during development. Doubling its 630-pixel width to 1260 recovered the provided-datasets phrase. The configurable minimum region width is 1260 pixels. This choice is based on one source image and does not establish a general OCR accuracy optimum. The final evaluation below uses that setting. The mind map source is 4403 by 7037 pixels; its visible labels are checked without claiming diagram understanding.

## Warnings and limits

Synthetic fixtures exercise known scenarios; they cannot establish arbitrary real-document accuracy. User images are checked for visible labels and the poster datasets sentence, not diagram relationships or semantics. Word/Excel coordinates remain synthetic. Low OCR confidence warnings do not fail a case when its required text is present.
